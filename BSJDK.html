BhoomiShakti JeevaDhara Krishi System

Excessive dependence on chemical fertilizers, particularly urea, has led to soil degradation, reduced microbial diversity, and a decline in earthworm populations. BhoomiShakti JeevaDhara (BSJD) is a controlled bio-nutrient soil system — aged manure, compost and diluted urine, mechanically mixed into the field with BhoomiShakti X1 — built to restore soil life without giving up nutrient efficiency.

Objective

Built to give the soil its life back

To develop a controlled bio-nitrogen system using urine, manure and compost that improves earthworm population and soil biological activity, enhances crop productivity sustainably, and reduces dependence on chemical fertilizers — using a mechanized solution (BhoomiShakti X1) for efficient soil mixing.

How JeevaDhara Works

The Working Principle

Aged manure, compost, soil and cocopeat are mixed dry, then a diluted urine solution (1:8) is added gradually and stabilized for 24–48 hours. During this time, urea from the urine converts into ammonium and then into nitrate by soil microorganisms, while the compost regulates the release — giving a controlled, non-toxic nutrient supply instead of a fast, harsh one. The stabilized mix is then loaded into BhoomiShakti X1, which moves across the field and uses rotary blades to mix it evenly into the soil.

System Overview

Key Features

Controlled Bio-Nitrogen Release

Urea from urine converts to ammonium, then nitrate, for steady nutrient availability instead of a toxic, fast release.

Simple, Proven Composition

Aged manure, compost, soil and cocopeat blended in a set ratio, wetted with diluted (1:8) urine.

Restores Earthworms & Soil Life

Supports earthworm population growth and microbial diversity within 4–8 weeks.

Mechanized With BhoomiShakti X1

Rotary blades ensure uniform incorporation into soil, cutting labour and time by an estimated 60–70%.

Better Soil Structure & Moisture

Improves aeration and moisture retention, alongside stable crop productivity.

Eco-Friendly & Cost-Effective

Reduces chemical dependency and pollution while supporting safer food and fodder.

Farms already using, or renting, BhoomiShakti X1 for mixing

Advantages

BhoomiShakti JeevaDhara is eco-friendly, cost-effective and improves soil fertility over the long term. It supports earthworm populations, reduces chemical pollution and greenhouse gas emissions, and helps produce safer food and fodder for people and animals.

Good to know: JeevaDhara is slower-acting than chemical fertilizers, needs 24–48 hours of preparation time before use, and works best with proper ongoing management. This system improves soil and health conditions — it does not directly cure diseases.
