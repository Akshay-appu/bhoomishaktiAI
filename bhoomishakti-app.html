Objective

One app, three roles, one connected ecosystem

To give farmers, agri-industry buyers and agri-startups a single connected app — layering an AI-powered Agriculture Intelligence Engine and a direct Farmer-Industry-Startup ecosystem on top of the already-live BhoomiShakti Agri Digital platform, so farm decisions, market access and technology adoption all happen in one place.

How The App Works

Getting Started, Step By Step

Every user starts by choosing a role — Farmer, Agri Industry or Agri Startup — then verifies their phone number with an OTP and completes a short profile. From there, each role lands on its own dashboard, built around what that role needs most: farm intelligence for farmers, sourcing tools for industry, and farmer access for startups — with direct messaging connecting all three.

App Overview

Key Features

Role-Based Dashboards

Separate command centers for Farmer, Agri Industry and Agri Startup, each built around what that role needs most.

AI Farm Assistant

A chat-based farm assistant with quick-question prompts, built to answer farm questions directly in the app.

Market Intelligence

Prices, industry demand and procurement opportunities in one Market tab, each clearly labelled live, recent or demo.

Direct Agri Ecosystem

Farmer, Industry and Startup accounts can message each other directly, with no intermediaries in between.

Innovation Hub & Pilots

Startups list new agri-technology; farmers discover it and request pilots suited to their own farm.

Farmer Passport

A digital agricultural identity showing crops, stats, a Farm Health Score and certifications.

Who It's For

Built For Three Roles, One Ecosystem

One app, whichever side of the ecosystem you're on.

Farmers wanting direct market access and AI-backed farm intelligence
Agri-businesses and industry buyers sourcing reliable, traceable produce
Agri startups looking for real farmers to pilot new technology
Anyone already using BhoomiShakti Agri Digital who wants a connected app experience

Advantages

One app brings farm intelligence, market access and technology adoption together for Farmer, Agri Industry and Agri Startup users — cutting down the number of separate tools, calls and middlemen it takes to run or grow an agricultural business.

Good to know: The BhoomiShakti App is currently a complete UI/UX prototype built to demonstrate the full experience with demo data. It builds directly on top of the already-live BhoomiShakti Agri Digital web platform; backend services and AI/ML integration are the proposed next phase, not yet live.
