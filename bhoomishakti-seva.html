How It Works

From Search to Service, in Eleven Simple Steps

A clear path from choosing a service to paying and rating it — built to feel simple on any phone.

Step 01

Choose

Select the service you need.

Step 02

Location

Choose your farm location.

Step 03

Find

Discover nearby service providers.

Step 04

Verify

View verified provider profile, photo, machine details, equipment and ratings.

Step 05

Book

Choose date and time and request the service.

Step 06

Accept

Provider accepts the booking.

Step 07

Track

View service/provider status and location when tracking is enabled.

Step 08

Work

Provider reaches the farm and starts work.

Step 09

Complete

Work is completed.

Step 10

Pay

Pay using PhonePe or Cash.

Step 11

Rate
