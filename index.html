About BhoomiShakti

One Ecosystem. Seven Innovations. A Shared Rural Vision.

Multiple Innovations, One Ecosystem

Built Around Rural Communities

BhoomiShakti is an integrated rural innovation ecosystem developed as a Smart India Hackathon initiative. Rather than a single product, it brings together seven purpose-built innovations that work together across farm machinery, sustainable agriculture, intelligence and rural services.

Seven interconnected innovations designed to work as one system.

Spans the rural value chain — from farm machinery to digital market access.

Centred on the needs of small and marginal farmers.

Built as a Smart India Hackathon (SIH) initiative.
