Objective

Built to make seed sowing lighter, faster and fuel-free

To design a portable, cost-effective, and eco-friendly seed-sowing solution that reduces manual effort and uneven seed distribution for small and marginal farmers, using simple mechanical design and renewable energy.

How S4 Works

The Working Principle

S4 is worn as a backpack unit with a seed storage bucket connected to dual dispensing pipes, letting farmers sow seeds uniformly on both sides while walking across the field. Vibration motors at the base keep seed flow continuous and clog-free, while an adjustable speed regulator controls the sowing rate for different crops.

Product Overview

Key Features

Wearable Backpack Design

A lightweight backpack unit that's easy to wear and carry while walking the field.

Dual Dispensing Pipes

Sows seeds uniformly on both sides of the farmer in a single pass across the field.

Clog-Free Seed Flow

Vibration motors at the base keep seeds flowing continuously without blockages.

Adjustable Sowing Rate

A speed regulator lets farmers control the sowing rate to suit different crops.

Dual Charging — Solar + Electric

A rechargeable battery charges from an integrated solar panel or a normal electric socket.

Shade While You Sow

The solar panel is mounted on an integrated umbrella, reducing heat stress during fieldwork.
