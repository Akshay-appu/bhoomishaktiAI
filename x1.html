Objective

Built to reduce fuel dependency on the farm

To design a solar-assisted, battery-powered agricultural machine that reduces fuel dependency, lowers operational costs, and improves efficiency for small-scale farming.

How X1 Works

The Working Principle

The system uses a solar panel to charge a rechargeable battery, which powers a DC motor. The motor drives a chain and sprocket mechanism, rotating the traction wheel and plough blade assembly — enabling efficient soil cutting and turning, even in narrow and small farm areas.

Advantages

BhoomiShakti X1 reduces fuel consumption, minimizes environmental impact, and lowers farming costs. It enhances productivity while reducing physical strain on farmers, making it ideal for sustainable agriculture.
